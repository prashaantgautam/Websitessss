An ongoing mountain transportation project involving trail alignment, drainage, masonry, soling, extensive stair construction and rest facilities in challenging terrain.

My involvement

My work on the project is field-oriented, with responsibilities around site measurement, construction monitoring, coordination, billing and progress documentation.
