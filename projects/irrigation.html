Irrigation Infrastructure

Pokhara Khola Magar Besare Khanitar Chhangtar Irrigation Project

Field and project engineering work on a lined irrigation system with canals, pipelines and hydraulic structures. My responsibilities included surveying, site supervision, measurement, quality checks and contractor billing.

Dibyashwori/Om JV · Aug 2025 – Mar 2026 · Project handed over

My involvement

I supported day-to-day field and project activities from survey and setting-out through construction monitoring, quantity measurement, quality checks and billing. The work required close attention to drawings, site conditions and the sequence of hydraulic and protection works.

Scope

Hydraulic infrastructure

  • Lined irrigation canals
  • Concrete-lined sections and pipeline sections
  • Syphons, VRB and super passages
  • Intake and reservoir tank

Protection & energy control

  • Water-dissipating structures
  • Gabion walls
  • Masonry walls
Responsibilities
  • Site supervision and construction monitoring
  • Surveying, setting-out and alignment-related field work
  • Auto Level and Total Station leveling and measurement
  • GPS-based ground profiling
  • Drawing interpretation and checking
  • Concrete, reinforcement, masonry/gabion and pipeline checks
  • Quantity measurement, BOQ checking and contractor billing
  • Measurement-book and progress documentation
  • Coordination with consultants/client representatives
  • Material quality/testing and construction quality checks
