About

A civil engineer with field experience and a clear research direction.

Professional profile

Transportation, structural assessment and resilient infrastructure.

My recent work spans irrigation infrastructure, mountain trail construction, tourism infrastructure and post-fire damage assessment. Across these projects, I have worked with surveying, site supervision, construction measurement, billing, testing, structural analysis, reporting and coordination.
